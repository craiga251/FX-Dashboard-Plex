Risk & correlation notes
Assume a generic FX portfolio with max 1% risk per trade, 3% total, and treat correlation explicitly:

USD‑major cluster (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD, NZD/USD, USD/CHF, USD/CAD, USD/JPY): All Top 5 trades are effectively short USD, so they are positively correlated; treat the whole USD‑short complex as one macro bet driven by DXY and US yields. Risk‑manage as if you had one large DXY short broken into legs, not as five separate, uncorrelated trades.

Antipodeans (AUD/USD, NZD/USD): These two are highly correlated, both sensitive to China, commodities and global risk sentiment. If you are long AUD/USD, size any NZD/USD long at most half‑risk or avoid it; do not allocate full 1% risk to both simultaneously.

Commodity‑linked FX (AUD, NZD, CAD): AUD and NZD benefit from broader risk and China stories, while CAD adds a strong oil component, making it partially independent. Holding AUD/USD long and USD/CAD short is reasonable, but consider capping combined risk in this “commodity bucket” to ~1.5–2% of capital.

Safe‑haven cluster (JPY, CHF vs USD): Both JPY and CHF benefit from risk‑off / lower‑yield environments, but CHF is more tightly linked to European risk while JPY is dominated by rate differentials and BoJ policy. If you run both USD/JPY shorts and USD/CHF shorts, treat them as moderately correlated; size each at ~0.5–0.7% to keep combined haven‑risk within limits.

Cross‑asset correlations:

Oil ↔ CAD: Further declines in WTI as Hormuz normalises would normally weigh on CAD, but the current story mixes lower oil with softer USD, partially offsetting effects; monitor WTI and EIA inventories if you’re in USDCAD.[page:usd22]

Equities ↔ JPY: High and rising risk appetite (S&P at records) tends to support USD/JPY; any abrupt equity sell‑off or ceasefire breakdown could quickly strengthen JPY and accelerate downside in USD/JPY.

Practical sizing guidance:

If you take GBP/USD long + AUD/USD long + USD/CAD short, treat that as ~3 legs of the same USD‑short theme; consider 0.7–0.8% risk each so total USD‑short risk is ~2–2.4%, leaving some room for a smaller USD/JPY or USD/CHF short.

Avoid being simultaneously long AUD, NZD and CAD all at 1% risk – pick 2 of the 3 or cut each to ~0.5% risk.
